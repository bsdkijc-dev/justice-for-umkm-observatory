# Survey Informed Consent Protocol

1. **Clear Purpose:** Inform prospective UMKM respondents that the survey is an independent academic/policy research initiative led by Cecep Mustafa, Ph.D., and is not a government enforcement or commercial program.
2. **Voluntary & Revocable:** Respondents may skip any question or withdraw at any time without penalty.
3. **Anonymity & De-Identification:** Public research datasets use only `respondent_code` and banded/aggregated attributes (`estimated_cost_band`, `estimated_duration_band`, `business_age_group`).
4. **Consent Verification:** Record `consent_status` (`consented_public_aggregate`, `consented_research_only`, `withdrawn`) before including any response in analysis.
