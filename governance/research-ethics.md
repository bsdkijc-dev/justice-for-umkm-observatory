# Research Ethics Policy — Justice for UMKM Observatory

1. **Voluntary Participation:** All survey respondents and interview participants must participate voluntarily with explicit, revocable informed consent.
2. **Do No Harm:** Research data must **never** be used to target, profile, or expose individual debtors, creditors, business owners, or litigants.
3. **No Automated Legal Advice:** The Observatory provides general legal information and empirical policy research only. It must **never** provide automated legal conclusions to individual businesses.
4. **Independent Governance:** Do not claim official endorsement by Mahkamah Agung RI, the Government of Indonesia, or any public institution without formal written authorization.
5. **Pre-Launch Survey Gate:** No survey or interview fieldwork may launch until instruments, consent forms, privacy safeguards, and sampling plans pass ethical review.
