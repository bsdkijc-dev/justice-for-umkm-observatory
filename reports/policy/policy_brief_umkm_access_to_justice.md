# Policy Intelligence Brief — Improving Legal Resilience of Indonesian UMKM

**Status:** Framework & Candidate Evaluation Matrix (Pending Empirical Validation)  
**Research Director:** Cecep Mustafa, Ph.D.

> **Governance Note:** Candidate proposals below are structured for evaluation against empirical evidence from `DS01`–`DS09`. Not every legal barrier requires new legislation; administrative, informational, and mediation improvements are prioritized alongside statutory options.

| # | Proposed Intervention | Institutional Responsibility | Legal Feasibility | Target Beneficiaries | Evaluation Indicators |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Plain-language contract guidance | Kementerian UMKM, BPHN | High (administrative) | Micro & small enterprises | IND01, IND10 |
| 2 | Standard small-business contract templates | Kementerian UMKM, KADIN/Associations | High (administrative) | B2B suppliers & service MSEs | IND05, IND10 |
| 3 | Accessible dispute prevention information | Kementerian UMKM, OSS | High (digital portal) | Newly formalized NIB holders | IND01, IND10 |
| 4 | Improved access to mediation ([PERMA 1/2016](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209641/perma-no-1-tahun-2016)) | Mahkamah Agung, Accredited Mediators | High (existing rule) | MSEs in civil/commercial disputes | IND07, IND08, IND04 |
| 5 | Affordable legal consultation | BPHN, Bar Associations, Clinics | Medium (budget/partnership) | Micro & small enterprises | IND02, IND03 |
| 6 | Better info on commercial remedies ([PERMA 4/2019](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/206070/perma-no-4-tahun-2019)) | Mahkamah Agung, District Courts | High (public education) | MSEs with claims <= IDR 500M | IND01, IND04 |
| 7 | Improved access to legal aid where eligible ([UU 16/2011](https://bphn.go.id/faq)) | BPHN, Accredited OBH | Medium (eligibility scope) | Indigent micro-entrepreneurs | IND02, IND11 |
| 8 | Clearer business licensing guidance | BKPM/OSS, Pemda | High (portal UX) | Informal & transitioning MSEs | IND01, IND10 |
| 9 | Better coordination of legal information | Kementerian UMKM, BPHN, MA | Medium (inter-agency) | All UMKM nationwide | IND10, IND11 |
| 10 | Improved public understanding of enforcement | Mahkamah Agung, District Courts | High (public guidance) | Winning claimants | IND09, IND12 |
