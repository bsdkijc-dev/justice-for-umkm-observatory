# UMKM Survey Sampling Plan (Draft — Pre-Launch Gate)

1. **Target Population:** Indonesian Micro and Small Enterprises (UMK) across urban and rural districts, with a secondary comparison stratum of Medium Enterprises.
2. **Stratification Strategy:** Stratified by island/region (Java, Sumatra, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, Bali-Nusa Tenggara, Maluku-Papua), urban vs. rural *kabupaten/kota*, and business size (`micro`, `small`, `medium`).
3. **Sample Size & Power:** Target minimum `n = 1,200` completed responses across >= 15 provinces for descriptive subgroup precision; convenience pilots (`n < 200`) must be explicitly labeled as non-representative exploratory pilots.
4. **Pre-Launch Requirements:** Ethical review sign-off, finalized consent form (`survey/consent_template.md`), PII-free data pipeline verification (`processing/privacy_review.py`), and documented non-response bias protocol.
