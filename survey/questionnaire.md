# UMKM Dispute Experience Survey Questionnaire (Draft — Pending Ethical Review)

> **GATE:** Do NOT deploy this instrument until ethical review, consent workflow, privacy safeguards, and sampling plan are approved.

## Module A: Enterprise Profile (Statutory PP 7/2021 Alignment)
- **A1.** `business_size`: Micro (capital <= IDR 1B or annual sales <= IDR 2B) / Small (capital >1B–5B or sales >2B–15B) / Medium (capital >5B–10B or sales >15B–50B) / Prefer not to say (`unknown`)
- **A2.** `business_sector`: KBLI / primary sector (Food & Beverage, Retail/Trade, Manufacturing/Craft, Services, Agriculture/Fishery, Digital/E-Commerce, Other)
- **A3.** `province` & `district` (Kabupaten/Kota only — no street address)
- **A4.** `business_age_group`: `<1_year`, `1_to_3_years`, `3_to_5_years`, `5_to_10_years`, `>10_years`

## Module B: Dispute Experience & Taxonomy (RQ01, RQ03)
- **B1.** `dispute_experience`: Has your business experienced a commercial, financial, property, consumer, regulatory, IP, or employment dispute in the past 24 months? (`yes` / `no`)
- **B2.** `dispute_category`: `CAT-01` through `CAT-08` (multiple selection permitted) or `UNKNOWN`
- **B3.** `resolution_channel`: `negotiation`, `mediation`, `administrative`, `arbitration`, `small_claims_court`, `general_court`, `abandoned_unresolved`, `other`

## Module C: Costs, Duration, & Operational Impact (RQ02, RQ05, RQ06)
- **C1.** `estimated_cost_band`: `0`, `<1M_IDR`, `1M_to_5M_IDR`, `5M_to_25M_IDR`, `25M_to_100M_IDR`, `>100M_IDR`, `unknown`
- **C2.** `estimated_duration_band`: `<1_month`, `1_to_3_months`, `3_to_6_months`, `6_to_12_months`, `>12_months`, `ongoing`
- **C3.** `business_impact`: `none`, `minor_delay`, `cash_flow_pressure`, `temporary_halt`, `workforce_reduction`, `closure_risk`
- **C4.** `outcome_category`: `settled_voluntarily`, `judgment_enforced`, `judgment_unenforced`, `ongoing`, `abandoned`, `dismissed`

## Module D: Consent & Metadata
- **D1.** `consent_status`: `consented_public_aggregate` / `consented_research_only` / `withdrawn`
- **D2.** `survey_date`: ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`
